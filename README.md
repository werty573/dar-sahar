# Dar Sahar — concept Arabian perfume house

A multi-page online store for **Dar Sahar** ("House of Dawn"), a fictional Trinidad & Tobago retailer of authentic Arabian fragrances, body sprays and decants.

Concept build by **Portside Digital**. Dar Sahar is not a real business. Fragrance names are the real Gulf-house perfumes such a shop would stock; all photography is illustrative, from [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com) (Unsplash License).

**Pages**
- `index.html` — scroll-driven hero: an Arabic-arch window opens to a full-bleed bottle shot, with drifting gold motes and smoke. Shop by mood, bestsellers, a sticky "Anatomy of Khamrah" notes walkthrough (top → heart → base), decants, Scent Finder teaser, promises, reviews, expo dates.
- `shop.html` — search, Her / Him / Unisex, scent-family chips, sale/new/body-spray/oils filters, sorting by price, projection or longevity. Quick-add modal.
- `product.html?id=…` — arch gallery with zoom, sizes (100ml / 10ml & 5ml decants / body spray), note pyramid, projection & longevity meters, how-to-wear, sticky mobile add bar.
- `finder.html` — 5-question Scent Finder (tuned for Caribbean heat) → three matches with % score; add all three as decants in one tap.
- `discovery.html` — build-your-own Discovery Box (3 / 5 / 8 × 5ml) with a case that fills with vials.
- `house.html` — story, authenticity promise, expo calendar with pre-order-for-pickup, delivery & payment, FAQ.

**Ordering:** a bag that persists across pages. "Send order on WhatsApp" pre-writes the full order (items, sizes, quantities, box contents, delivery area & fee, total, name). The floating WhatsApp button always carries context: the current product, the box being built, the finder matches, or the bag.

No real orders are placed and no payments are taken. All data stays in the visitor's browser.
