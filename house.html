Our story

It started with one bottle from Dubai.

A gift of Khamrah from a cousin abroad turned into friends asking "where can I get that?" — and nobody locally could promise it was real. So we started importing direct from the Gulf houses ourselves.

Today we're a registered T&T perfume business carrying the Arabian fragrances everyone is talking about — full bottles, body sprays and hand-poured decants — delivered to every corner of the islands, and on the tester table at expos most months.

FAQ

Good questions

Are Arabian perfumes long-lasting in our heat?

Yes — that's why people switch. Most of our fragrances are high-concentration Eau de Parfum built on amber, musk and oud bases that hold up to Caribbean humidity far better than many designer scents. Spray on clothes too; fabric holds them for days.

What's a decant and is it the real thing?

A decant is the exact same perfume, hand-poured from a sealed full bottle into a clean glass atomiser. 5ml is about 70 sprays — enough to wear it for a couple of weeks and decide.

How do I order?

Add what you like to your bag and tap "Send order on WhatsApp". Your full order arrives in our chat, we confirm stock and delivery day, and you pay by transfer, Linx or cash on delivery. Or just message us — we're happy to help you choose.

Do you do gifts and corporate orders?

Yes. Free gift wrap with a handwritten note on every order, and we put together custom Discovery Boxes and bulk gifting for weddings, Eid, Divali, Christmas and corporate events.

Can I return a perfume?

Sealed, unopened bottles can be exchanged within 7 days. For hygiene reasons opened bottles and decants can't be returned — that's exactly why we offer decants first.
